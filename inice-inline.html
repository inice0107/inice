About InIce

InIce，以喜马拉雅山麓的古老草本智慧为根基，将七脉轮的能量疗愈融入每一支线香。我们相信，香气是通往内在的桥梁——当一缕烟升起，外界的喧嚣便如潮水般退去，只剩下此刻的宁静与觉知。

Rooted in the ancient herbal wisdom of the Himalayan foothills, InIce weaves the energy healing of the seven chakras into every incense stick. We believe fragrance is a bridge to the inner self — as the smoke rises, the noise of the world recedes like a tide, leaving only presence and stillness.

手工制香

Handcrafted with Intention

每一支线香皆源自尼泊尔加德满都谷地的传统手工坊。选用喜马拉雅山区野生草本与天然树脂，依循千年阿育吠陀配方，以手工揉制、自然风干，保留草本纤维的原始肌理。

Each stick is born in a traditional workshop in Nepal's Kathmandu Valley. Wild Himalayan herbs and natural resins are blended according to ancient Ayurvedic recipes, hand-rolled and sun-dried to preserve the raw texture of the fibers.
